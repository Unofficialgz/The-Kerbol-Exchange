The Kerbol Exchange 1.0.0
Fortunes beyond Kerbin.

An Ore market for KSP career mode. Mine stock Ore on planets, moons, asteroids or comets, then bring it back to Kerbin to sell at the current price.

The Kerbol Exchange is an unofficial, independently developed mod for Kerbal Space Program. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the game's developers or publishers.

Requirements
KSP 1.12.5, Harmony 2 (tested with 2.2.1.0) and Click Through Blocker (tested with 2.1.10.23). Install dependencies separately.

Installation
Close KSP and extract the player ZIP into your KSP folder so GameData/KerbolExchange is created. Back up customized Prices.cfg before replacing the old mod folder, then restore it. Remove a backed-up old OreMarket installation. Keep only one installation and price configuration.

Mining and selling
Use supported stock drills and Ore tanks. Recover tracked cargo on Kerbin in career mode to sell at the current market price, alongside the normal vessel refund. Prices update every 60 seconds while you play. Pausing stops the timer; time warp does not speed it up.

Preloaded or untracked Ore earns no bonus. Each normal tank holds one origin; transfers require an empty tank or matching origin. Kerbin Ore has no sale value. Whole objects and unmined reserves cannot be sold. Unsupported resource operations may invalidate affected cargo. Science and Sandbox modes do not award sales.

Planet packs require compatible stock harvesting and globally unique internal body names. Multi-star gameplay testing remains incomplete. Configuration guide: PlanetPacks.txt.
